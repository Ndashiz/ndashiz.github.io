Cookies & privacy

No tracking cookies. Here is exactly what happens instead.

This page lists everything the site stores on your device, what it measures, and where your details go when you write to me. It is written for humans, not lawyers — and it is complete.

What is stored on your device

The site sets no cookie of its own. It keeps three small values in your browser's storage, none of which identifies you or leaves your device:

KeyWhat it holdsHow long
lg_langYour language choice (EN or FR), so the page opens in the language you picked.Until you clear your browser storage.
lg_popA flag saying the “need more information?” pop-up has already been shown, so it is not shown twice.Until you close the tab.
lg_crewOnly on my own devices: a flag that excludes my test visits from the statistics. It is never set for a visitor.Until cleared.

The site is served through Cloudflare, which may set a short-lived security cookie (__cf_bm, about 30 minutes) to tell browsers from bots. It is strictly necessary to serve the page and contains no personal data.

Audience measurement — first party, no third party

To know whether the site works, it sends a handful of anonymous events to my own server (jarvis.ndashiz.be) — not to Google Analytics, not to any advertising network:

  • the page was loaded; which sections scrolled into view; how far down you scrolled;
  • which buttons were clicked; whether the contact pop-up was opened, and by what;
  • how many seconds the tab stayed visible, so I can tell a glance from a read;
  • the language, and whether you are on a phone, a tablet or a desktop;
  • the domain of the site that sent you here (never the full address), reduced to a category such as “LinkedIn” or “Google” before it is stored.

These events share a random session number that lives in the tab's memory and dies with it: a reload is a new session, and nothing links two visits together. No IP address, no browser fingerprint, no user agent and no cookie are stored. Visit lines are deleted after 30 days; daily totals are kept for 12 months.

If your browser sends the Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the site sends a single anonymous page count and nothing else — no session, no timing, no referrer.

When you write to me

The contact form sends your name, e-mail, subject, phone number (optional), availability and message to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-e-mail service that delivers it to my inbox, and a copy to my own server so I can follow up. Both are used for one thing only: calling you back about your request.

I never share these details, never add them to a mailing list, and delete them on request.

Third parties the page talks to

  • Google Fonts — the three typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, which therefore see your IP address when the page loads. No cookie is set.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare — the hosting and the network in front of it keep standard server logs for security.
  • FormSubmit — only when you send the contact form (see above).
